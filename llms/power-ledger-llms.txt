# Powerledger

> Powerledger (Powerledger AG / Power Ledger Pty Ltd, Perth, Western Australia) builds
> software for tracking, tracing and trading renewable energy and environmental commodities.
> It sells a modular platform to utilities, retailers, network operators, generators and
> corporates. **Powerledger publishes no public API, no developer portal, and no
> machine-readable API contract.** This file is generated by API Evangelist from a probe of
> Powerledger's public surface on 2026-08-26; it is not published by Powerledger.

## What Powerledger sells

- xGrid — peer-to-peer trading of solar power across the grid, between customers of the same
  or different retailers, with agile real-time pricing.
- uGrid — behind-the-meter energy trading inside a single site, building or embedded network.
- TraceX — a digital marketplace for Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs) and other Energy
  Attribute Certificates, attribute-agnostic and extending to carbon and thermal certificates.
  Integrated with M-RETS (largest North American voluntary REC registry) and ERCOT.
- Vision / PPA Vision — 24/7 renewable energy portfolio and power-purchase-agreement tracking.
- Hydrogen and EV settlement products.

## Machine-readable surface — what exists

- No OpenAPI, Swagger, GraphQL SDL, AsyncAPI, JSON Schema, Postman collection, MCP server or
  A2A agent card is published on any Powerledger host.
- api.powerledger.io resolves but returns HTTP 502 on every path probed.
- docs.powerledger.io and developer.powerledger.io do not resolve.
- The Powerledger Energy Blockchain (a permissioned Solana-derived chain) exposed a
  Solana-compatible JSON-RPC entrypoint at https://powr-api.mainnet.powerledger.io, named in
  the validator README at https://github.com/PowerLedger/powr. It did not answer an
  anonymous probe on 2026-08-26, and Powerledger has publicly stated it has moved from its
  own chain to Solana mainnet.
- POWR is now an SPL token on Solana mainnet-beta, mint
  PowerhfyX6JqFprXzy89fPBSKqmLj8Yzcvr3FkHrQHR. Anything programmatic about POWR runs through
  standard Solana RPC, not through a Powerledger endpoint.
